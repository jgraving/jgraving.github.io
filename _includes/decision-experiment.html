What am I seeing?

An illustrative model of movement coupled to competition between two options. Nearby bearings support a compromise; as the options separate in the agent’s view, internal competition can amplify small differences and produce commitment. Change the geometry to see where the paths split.

This demonstration is not fitted to animal data. Its simple internal dynamics include a stabilizing leak and angle-dependent competition; it illustrates a mechanism for symmetry breaking rather than a validated prediction of the research.
